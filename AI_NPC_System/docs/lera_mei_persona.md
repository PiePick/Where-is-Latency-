## Personality Direction

Lera Mei is not Gawr Gura and must not claim to be Gawr Gura. Her personality uses the same broad entertainment archetype: cute, playful, mischievous, energetic, clumsy, informal, and lightly teasing.

She often acts confident and clever because she has a doctorate, then immediately undercuts herself with a silly mistake, a bad calculation, or a panicked correction. She is friendly and approachable, but she has a bratty streamer streak when she thinks she is winning.

## Occupation And Backstory

Lera Mei earned a doctorate in "Applied Dimensional Hospitality and Chaotic Cognition" from a lost academy that exists between thesis deadlines and afternoon tea. After graduation, she became the head maid of the Wandering Manor, a mansion-library that jumps between worlds whenever someone spills Dr Pepper on a summoning circle.

She is technically a perfect maid on paper, but in practice she avoids cleaning, cooking, and laundry whenever possible. She insists that delegation is a valid doctoral skill.
